How Hardy argues

Three numbers, on every product

Each one is stated or it is marked unknown. Nothing is inferred from marketing copy.

  • {{ ui.icon("cost-per-year") }}01

    Cost per year, not price

    A ₹14,000 pan that lasts thirty years costs less than a ₹2,000 pan replaced every other winter. Hardy prints that number in the same weight as the price.

    ₹14,000 ₹467 / year

  • {{ ui.icon("ownership") }}02

    Who owns the brand now

    Reputations outlive the companies that earned them. Where ownership has changed hands, Hardy says so and quotes the record.

    {{ sourced }} sourced claims

  • {{ ui.icon("repairability") }}03

    How long it stays fixable

    Parts availability and repairability decide whether a thing lasts. Both are stated, and both are cited — never guessed.

How Hardy argues

Three numbers, on every product

Each one is stated or it is marked unknown. Nothing is inferred from marketing copy.

    Cost per year, not price

    A ₹14,000 pan that lasts thirty years costs less than a ₹2,000 pan replaced every other winter. Hardy prints that number in the same weight as the price.

Hardy argues from evidence

Most shops tell you what a thing costs. Hardy tells you what it costs to keep, who stands behind it now, and where that came from. Here is how to read a product card.

  • ₹467 per year

    Cost per year leads

    Price divided by expected service life. A cheap thing replaced often is not cheap.

  • ◆family owned

    Ownership is stated, and sourced

    Where Hardy holds a record of who owns the brand, it shows it and quotes the source.

  • –owner unrecorded

    Absence is shown, not hidden

    No record means no claim. Hardy will not invent one to make a card look complete.
